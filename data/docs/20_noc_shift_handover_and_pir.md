# Runbook 20 (SYNTHETIC): NOC Shift Handover and Post-Incident Review

## Shift handover
Shifts overlap by 30 minutes. The handover note lists open P1 and P2 incidents, ongoing
changes, pending field dispatches, and known degraded cells.

## Post-incident review (PIR)
- Required for every P1 and P2.
- Hold the review within 5 business days of resolution.
- Publish the written report within 10 business days.
- Blameless format. Sections: timeline, root cause, impact (subscribers and duration),
  what went well, and actions with an owner and a due date.
