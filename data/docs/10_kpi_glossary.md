# Runbook 10 (SYNTHETIC): KPI Glossary and Baselines

- PRB utilization: used PRBs divided by available PRBs, times 100.
- RRC SSR: successful RRC setups divided by RRC setup attempts.
- Handover success rate: successful handovers divided by handover attempts.
- Cell availability target: 99.9%.
- Baseline: the median of the same hour-of-week over the previous 4 weeks.
- Busy hour: the hour with the highest traffic in the day.
- RSRP: good above -90 dBm, fair between -90 and -105 dBm, poor below -105 dBm.
- SINR: poor below 0 dB.
