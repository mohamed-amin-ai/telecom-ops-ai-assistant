# Runbook 12 (SYNTHETIC): Power and Environment Alarms

## Mains failure
Every site must sustain at least 4 hours on batteries.
- A single-site mains failure is P3 while the battery is above 50%.
- Raise to P2 if the battery falls below 30%, or if 5 or more sites in the same area lose
  mains power.
- Dispatch a generator when the battery is below 40% and mains power is not restored.

## Temperature
A cabinet temperature above 45 degrees C raises the high-temperature alarm. Above 55
degrees C there is a thermal shutdown risk: dispatch the field team immediately.

## Rectifier and DC voltage
- A single rectifier module failure is P4, or P3 if the remaining capacity cannot carry
  the load.
- On a -48 V plant, a DC voltage below 46 V raises the low-voltage alarm.

## Door open
Verify a door-open alarm against the access log. If the access is unauthorized, notify the
Security team.
